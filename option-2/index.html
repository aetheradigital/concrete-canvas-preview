  2. 2GrindDiamond-grinding opens the concrete so the coating bonds in.

Questions

Good to know.

Will it hold up to Florida sun on my lanai or pool deck?

Outdoor floors get a UV-stable topcoat (typically polyaspartic) that won’t yellow the way plain epoxy can. For pool decks we add grip and recommend lighter colors that stay cooler underfoot.

What about hot tire pickup in the garage?

Grinding the slab and using a high-build system is what stops hot tires from lifting the coating. We spec the system for the space when we visit.

Is it slippery when it’s wet?

Any finish can take an anti-slip additive, and quartz floors are textured by design — a favorite for pool decks, outdoor showers and kitchens.

How long until I can use it?

It depends on the system and the weather that day. Your written quote lists exact walk-on and drive-on times.

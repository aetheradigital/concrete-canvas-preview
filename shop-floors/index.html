Quick Answers

Before You Book.

Can you really finish over a weekend?

For most shops, yes. We confirm cure times for your system and plan the schedule in the quote.

Will it hold up to jack stands and lifts?

Shop systems are built thick for point loads, hot tires and dropped tools. We spec it for your equipment.

What about oil that’s already soaked in?

We grind and clean the slab first. Heavily contaminated areas get extra prep before coating.

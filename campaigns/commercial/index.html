Campaign ideas beyond the feed

Commercial buyers want proof and a relationship. These build both.

Direct mail

The floor report card

Mail facility managers a one-page “floor report card” checklist (slips, cracks, chemical stains) with a QR code to book an assessment.

Partnerships

Contractor & architect lunch

Host a lunch-and-learn for general contractors and architects on specifying coatings for Florida slabs.

Case study

Overnight install time-lapse

Film a phased overnight install and turn it into a case study page, a LinkedIn video and a sales leave-behind.

Email

Inspection season reminders

Quarterly emails to past and prospective clients ahead of health and safety inspection periods.

Launch notes

What it takes to run this for real. Budgets, offers and numbers in brackets are for Concrete Canvas to set.

Targeting

  • LinkedIn: facilities, operations and property managers; owners of restaurants, breweries, warehouses in Lee & Collier
  • Search: high-intent commercial terms only
  • Exclude residential terms

Budget & schedule

  • [$/month] LinkedIn, [$/month] search
  • Run 8–12 weeks (longer B2B buying cycle)
  • Follow up every lead within one business day

Measure

  • Cost per site assessment
  • Assessment-to-proposal rate
  • Pipeline value and close rate

Needs from Concrete Canvas

  • Commercial project photos and any certifications
  • Confirm after-hours and phasing availability
  • A one-page capabilities sheet for sales follow-up

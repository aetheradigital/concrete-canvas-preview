Your Bay Is Your Billboard.

A campaign for independent auto shops, detailers, tire and service centers. Customers judge a shop by what they see, and the floor is the biggest thing in the room. Ads show oil wiping right off and promise a weekend install so no bays are lost.

Campaign Ideas Beyond the Feed

Shop owners buy from people they meet. Mix the ads with some old-fashioned legwork.

Drop-Off

The Rag Kit

Drop a branded shop rag and a small floor sample at local shops with a card: “Spill something on this. Then call us.”

Referral

Shop-to-Shop Referral

Give past shop clients a [$X] credit or a free annual recoat for every shop they refer.

Partnerships

Supplier Counter Cards

Ask local parts suppliers to keep a counter card with a QR code for the free walkthrough.

Social

Bay of the Month

Feature a client shop each month with before/after photos. They share it, and their customers see you.

Launch Notes

What it takes to run this for real. Budgets, offers and numbers in brackets are for Concrete Canvas to set.

Targeting

  • Facebook: owners and managers of auto repair, tire, detailing and body shops in Lee & Collier
  • Search: shop-specific terms
  • Exclude DIY garage terms

Budget & Schedule

  • [$/day] for 6 weeks
  • Drop-offs: 10 shops per week alongside the ads
  • Avoid early-week mornings, when shops are busiest

Measure

  • Cost per walkthrough
  • Walkthrough-to-job rate
  • Referrals from shop clients

Needs From Concrete Canvas

  • Confirm weekend install capacity and cure times for shop use
  • Set the referral credit
  • Photos from any shop jobs

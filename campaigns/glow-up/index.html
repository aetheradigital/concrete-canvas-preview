Your Garage. But Make It Art.

A bright, aspirational campaign that sells the transformation, not the coating. Ads reveal finished floors behind rising garage doors and cycling colors, then send homeowners to a page where they can design their floor and book a free in-home design visit.

Campaign Ideas Beyond the Feed

Low-cost ways to put finished floors in front of people while the paid ads run.

Yard Sign

Glow-Up in Progress

A bold sign on every install: “This garage is getting a glow-up. Yours could be next.” with a QR code to the floor designer.

Social

Door-Reveal Reels

Film every finished job from inside the garage as the door rolls up. Same shot every time builds a recognizable series.

Event

Sample Saturday

Set up a table of finish samples at a local home show or hardware store and book design visits on the spot.

Organic

Color of the Month

Feature one color each month across social, email and the website, with the floor designer preset to that color.

Launch Notes

What it takes to run this for real. Budgets, offers and numbers in brackets are for Concrete Canvas to set.

Targeting

  • Homeowners in Lee & Collier counties, ages 30–65
  • Interests: home improvement, garage organization, cars, DIY
  • Exclude existing customers (upload list)

Budget & Schedule

  • [$/day] split 70% social, 30% search
  • Run 4 weeks, then keep the two best ads
  • Refresh creative colors monthly (no new design work needed)

Measure

  • Cost per booked design visit
  • Landing page form rate (aim for steady week-over-week gains)
  • Visit-to-sale close rate from the CRM

Needs From Concrete Canvas

  • Approve the free design visit offer
  • Phone number, domain and service area
  • Real job photos and video to swap in over time

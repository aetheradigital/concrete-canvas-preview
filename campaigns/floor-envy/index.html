Campaign Ideas Beyond the Feed

This campaign works best when every finished floor recruits the street around it.

Door Hanger

The Neighborhood Notice

Print the “excessive shine” notice as a door hanger for the 20 homes around each install, with a QR code to the bundle page.

Nextdoor

Street Spotlight Post

Post a before/after from the street (with the homeowner’s OK) and mention the neighbor bundle in the comments.

Referral

Open-Door Weekend

Ask happy customers to leave the garage open on a Saturday morning with a small sign: “Ask me about my floor.”

Social

Floor-Off Polls

Post two floors side by side and let followers vote. The winner’s color becomes that month’s featured blend.

Launch Notes

What it takes to run this for real. Budgets, offers and numbers in brackets are for Concrete Canvas to set.

Targeting

  • 1-mile radius around each install from the last 90 days
  • Homeowners, ages 28–70
  • Nextdoor: neighborhoods with recent jobs

Budget & Schedule

  • [$/day] per active install radius, for 3 weeks after each job
  • Door hangers within 3 days of each install
  • Keep the humor light and never name real neighbors

Measure

  • Cost per quote
  • Share of quotes that add a neighbor
  • Jobs per street (clustering lowers travel cost)

Needs From Concrete Canvas

  • Set the neighbor bundle amount and rules
  • Customer permission to mention their street
  • Phone number and domain

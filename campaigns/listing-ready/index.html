Launch Notes

What it takes to run this for real. Budgets, offers and numbers in brackets are for Concrete Canvas to set.

Targeting

  • Homeowners in Lee & Collier showing moving or home-selling interest
  • Real estate agents (job title targeting)
  • Search: pre-listing and resale terms

Budget & Schedule

  • [$/day] social, [$/day] search, ongoing
  • Agent outreach: 2 brokerages per week
  • Peak push before spring selling season

Measure

  • Cost per quote
  • Jobs completed before listing date
  • Agent referrals per month

Needs From Concrete Canvas

  • Confirm 24-hour quote turnaround
  • Neutral color shortlist for sellers
  • Agent referral terms, if any

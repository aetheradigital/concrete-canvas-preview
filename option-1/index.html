10 — FAQ

Straight Answers.

Epoxy, polyaspartic or cementitious — what’s the difference?

Epoxy builds a thick, hard base with rich color. Polyaspartic topcoats cure fast and resist UV, so they suit sunny garages and outdoor slabs. Cementitious systems — overlays and urethane cement — rebuild worn concrete or stand up to heat and washdowns in commercial spaces. We often combine them.

How long until I can use the floor?

It depends on the system and the conditions on the day. We give you exact foot-traffic and vehicle return times in your written quote.

Will it work on my old, cracked slab?

Usually, yes. Grinding and repair are part of every job. Heavily damaged slabs may get a cementitious overlay first to rebuild the surface.

Is a coated floor slippery?

Every system can take an anti-slip additive, and quartz broadcast floors are textured by design for wet areas like pool decks and kitchens.
